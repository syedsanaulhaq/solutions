Simple Demo Overview

ECP AI Trainer

A simple digital training system for new ECP staff. It explains the agenda, answers questions, speaks back, and is available as two separate agents: one in English and one in Urdu.

Two Separate Agents Voice + Text Official Agenda Based Available 24/7
The Problem

What Problem Does It Solve?

New staff need quick guidance

Many new employees need the same orientation, but trainers are not always available.

Documents are long and difficult

Agendas, rules, and procedures are important, but they are hard to digest quickly.

People ask repeated questions

Basic training questions repeat again and again in every batch.

Consistency matters

ECP needs one standard explanation for everyone.

The Idea

What Is This System?

Think of it as two smart training assistants

Instead of waiting for a trainer, a new employee can open one of two pages: the English trainer or the Urdu trainer, then ask questions in a natural way.

How It Works

The Whole Journey in 4 Simple Steps

1

Open the page

The employee opens either the English trainer or the Urdu trainer.

2

Ask by text or voice

They type or speak a question in the language of that page.

3

AI explains simply

The system reads the agenda and answers in simple, non-technical language.

4

Learning happens instantly

The employee gets guidance immediately, without waiting for a trainer.

For the Training Wing

Keep answers focused
