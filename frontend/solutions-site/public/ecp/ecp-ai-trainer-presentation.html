Training Curriculum

Full 5-Day Onboarding Programme

  • Day 1: Joining formalities, ECP structure & organogram
  • Day 2: Electoral Roll — registration, revision & verification
  • Day 3: General Elections — RO/DRO roles, polling process
  • Day 4: By-Elections, Senate, Political Finance & code of conduct
  • Day 5: EDR technology, EMS system, ECP IT infrastructure
  • Constitutional & legal framework (Elections Act 2017)
  • Voter Registration Act & electoral rolls
  • Gender inclusion & disability representation
  • Media outreach & public awareness
  • ECP international relations & observer missions
  • Practical admin procedures & OMR forms
